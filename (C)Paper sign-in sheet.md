## Notes for Derek (not printed)

1. **Put the sheet on a clipboard at the door**, with the pen on a string. Point at the first line and say: "Sign in so I can send you the details. Phone number is the important one."
2. **The "I agree (initial)" column is the consent.** No initials, no follow-up of any kind. Do not text or call someone who left it blank, even if they gave a number. If they are interested, ask them to initial it.
3. **Keep the original sheets.** Do not throw them away, even after you enter the names. They are the proof of consent. Keep them somewhere private, not on the kitchen table.
4. **Do not send bulk texts from your own phone** to the whole list until the consent wording is lawyer-approved. A single personal follow-up to someone who initialled is the safe use.
5. **If someone asks "how do you use this?"** answer in one line: "Just to send you this listing and follow up, nothing else. Tell me to stop any time."
6. **If someone refuses to give a number**, let them look around. Offer the listing flyer or the listing page link instead. Never push.
7. **Two sheets minimum**, so you do not run out. One at the door, one spare.
8. **At the end**, count the rows, photograph each sheet (front only) for your own records, and give the originals to Philip or keep them in one folder.

## Open points for the lawyer

These are the same issues as questions 17 and 18 in `(C)Lawyer brief.md`, plus new ones this sheet raises:

- Is an initialled column enough as express consent for a text, or does it need a separate signature line? Does one tick cover phone, text and email together?
- This sheet does not say which messages will be sent. Is "details about this home and similar homes" enough, or must the sheet list each type of message?
- Does the sheet need the brokerage's mailing address and Derek's licence details to be valid contact information?
- If the paper details are later entered into ScanIn, does the paper consent still count as the record, and what must be kept (the original, a photo, or only the typed entry)?
- How long may Derek keep the sheets? 24 months is the current default; the lawyer should confirm.
- The buying-timeline and "working with an agent" columns: any limits under TRESA / RECO on following up with someone who says they already have an agent?
